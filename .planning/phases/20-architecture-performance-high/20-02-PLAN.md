**SettingsView.testConnection (ARCH-10):**
Lines ~100-105 wrap `saveServerSettings()` and `await viewModel.testConnection()` in an inline `Task {}`. Move this to the ViewModel:
1. In `SettingsViewModel`, add `func triggerTestConnection(serverURL: String, appState: AppState)` that internally creates a Task to update the URL and test.

**SettingsConfigSection.hookEventBreakdown (ARCH-11):**
